Our Journey

Our Story

Founded over 5 years ago, SME CONSULT (Safe Mine Entry & Exit) was established by a team of experienced mining safety professionals who recognised the critical need for comprehensive entry and exit monitoring in mining operations.

What started as a consultancy focused on safety protocols has evolved into a technology-driven solution provider. Today, we offer real-time worker monitoring systems, comprehensive analytics dashboards and integrated safety management platforms used by mining operations worldwide.

Our technology tracks over 10,000 workers daily, giving mining companies complete visibility of their workforce. From the moment a worker enters a mining pit to when they safely exit, our systems ensure full accountability and compliance.

As we look to the future, we remain committed to innovation and our core mission: ensuring that every mine worker is tracked, protected, and returns home safely at the end of their shift.
