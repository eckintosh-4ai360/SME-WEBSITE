Every worker carries an RFID badge that is scanned at each pit gate. Our system logs each entry and exit instantly, updating a live dashboard with who is on site, where, and for how long. Unusual activity — such as a missed exit — triggers immediate alerts to your safety team.

Our dashboard covers safety metrics, incident reports, compliance data and trend analysis. You get shift summaries, attendance patterns, certification status and audit-ready reports — all in one place, accessible from any device.
